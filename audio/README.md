# Audio Outputs

This folder contains audio outputs generated during the denoising process.

## Files

- `audio_noisy1.wav` — Noisy audio for Audio 1
- `audio_noisy2.wav` — Noisy audio for Audio 2
- `audio_sg1.wav` — Audio 1 after Spectral Gating
- `audio_sg2.wav` — Audio 2 after Spectral Gating
- `audio_wiener1.wav` — Audio 1 after Wiener Filter
- `audio_wiener2.wav` — Audio 2 after Wiener Filter

The audio processing and denoising procedures are implemented in the main notebook.
